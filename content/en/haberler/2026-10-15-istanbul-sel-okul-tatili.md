## Context

Istanbul is geographically prone to flash flooding after heavy downpours; the city's hilly topography, steep narrow streets, and inadequate stormwater infrastructure in some neighborhoods mean that even brief intense rainfall quickly disrupts traffic and daily life. Dense urbanization across a population exceeding fifteen million on both the European and Asian sides of the city compounds drainage challenges; older districts with limited rain-sewer capacity cannot absorb large volumes that fall in short periods.
