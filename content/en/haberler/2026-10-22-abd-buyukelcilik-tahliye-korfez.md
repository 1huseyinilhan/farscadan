According to Hamshahri, citing AFP, the US ordered embassy personnel and their families in Saudi Arabia to leave the country. The report is attributed to a diplomatic source and does not provide a specific date for when the order was issued. IRNA also relayed the same AFP report, characterizing the source as "a diplomatic source."

Donya-ye Eqtesad presented the issue within a different frame, citing Reuters: according to unnamed sources quoted by the news agency, the US State Department is "preparing" for some embassy personnel and their families in Persian Gulf countries to receive evacuation orders—meaning that the Reuters version Donya-ye Eqtesad reported describes a preparatory phase, not a completed process. According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, AFP also reported that embassy staff and family members at the United Arab Emirates, Jordan, Qatar, and Bahrain missions received evacuation orders. The same report mentioned that Israeli newspaper Haaretz claimed that a US-Israeli strike on Iran could begin within the next 72 hours; this claim has not been verified or confirmed by any other source.

Ettelaat addressed the issue with the question "Is the US evacuating its forces and diplomats from Middle East bases?" and reported, citing some sources, that a reduction in the number of US military personnel in the region's bases is occurring at a time when Donald Trump is evaluating a new military action against Iran. The reports were distributed on Wednesday (29 Mehr 1405 / October 21, 2026); no official statement from the US State Department was directly quoted in the Iranian press coverage.
