Tom Barrack, US Ambassador to Ankara and special envoy for Syria-Iraq, responded with "absolutely" when asked whether an attack on Iran might occur this coming weekend. According to Khabaronline, Barrack said: "President Trump has been completely forthright; he is ready and available to reach a deal. We have been at the negotiating table for months. Everyone is now agreed that an imminent nuclear threat has been eliminated."

According to Khabaronline's report, Barrack's remarks came as Trump continued making contradictory statements about Iran. According to Donya-e Eqtesad, Trump said on Thursday: "We are not negotiating with Iranians; very soon, something will happen, most people think we will take action in Iran after the election," then the next day offered different language: "We have a plan to negotiate with Iran, we will not attack them before the Congressional elections." According to Donya-e Eqtesad, the Atlantic magazine reported the Trump administration is considering a military operation lasting days after the midterm elections, with possible targets including Iran's missile and drone arsenals, energy infrastructure, and nuclear facilities; in the same report, DropSite noted that over the past four months Trump has backed away from a comprehensive operation against Iran or the Houthis seven times.

## Context

Tom Barrack has served as US Ambassador to Ankara since May 2025 and simultaneously holds the title of special envoy for Syria and Iraq; this week he drew attention at the Doha Global Security Forum in Qatar for saying he does not view Iran as a "bad regime." US-Iran negotiations have been stalled for months, and uncertainty persists about whether a ceasefire will become permanent. As US Congressional midterms approach, public debate in Washington centers on how Iran policy is being shaped by the election calendar; Barrack's remarks should be read as part of that discussion, though no official confirmation of any attack decision has been made.
