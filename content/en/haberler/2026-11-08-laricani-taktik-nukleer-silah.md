According to Khabaronline, Iranian politician and academic Mohammad Javad Larijani said on state television Sedavesima that nuclear weapons cannot be used against civilians but can be used "tactically" against an enemy military base. Larijani is identified in the report as head of the Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM); he is a conservative politician and former diplomat. Larijani's remarks on Friday, aired on state television, represented a rare, openly stated formulation in Iranian public discourse on nuclear weapons use.

According to Khabaronline's account, Larijani said: "Nuclear weapons cannot be used against civilians, but it is possible to use them tactically against an enemy military base. When you have a weapon in hand that can completely destroy an enemy military base, you use it; whether the weapon is nuclear or non-nuclear is not that important." Hamshahri carried the same quotation, attributing Larijani's words separately; both outlets reported the statement verbatim identically and added no editorial commentary.

According to Khabaronline's report, Larijani also criticized the current framework of Iran's nuclear debate and said "the discussion about whether to dilute uranium two kilograms or three kilograms must be closed; our nuclear debate should really be about why we should be in the NPT." Larijani argued that the NPT has not delivered the protections Iran was promised; saying "if the Agency (IAEA) comes and provides support, that is one thing, but if it does not provide support and comes whenever it wants and inspects wherever it wants and acts like a creditor, why should we remain in the NPT," Larijani asked. According to Khabaronline's report, Larijani discussed what he called "geographic constraint removal"—a strategy whereby if the U.S. uses its bases in Germany, Albania, or England against Iran, those third-country bases would also become legitimate Iranian targets. Khabaronline did not add explanation or verification when carrying these remarks; whether the statement signaled an actual military doctrine change was not indicated.

According to a separate Khabaronline report, sourcing the student news agency Daneşju, Larijani said that the real issue in Washington's political disagreement is not the principle of striking Iran, but Donald Trump's defeat and Iran's "victory." This statement was carried by Khabaronline from Daneşju and has not been independently verified by Farsçadan.

## How the Persian press covered it

Larijani's remarks on nuclear weapons appeared in at least two sources with the same quotation, but framing differed. Khabaronline (identified as aligned with Larijani's political circle) reported the story under the headline "nuclear weapons cannot be used against civilians but can be used tactically against enemy base," highlighting the NPT criticism and the U.S. bases threat in the same report—that is, treating it as one extended statement. Hamshahri, aligned with Tehran municipality and principlist ideology, carried the same quotation only under the headline "tactical nuclear weapons use against enemy military base" and left out the NPT and U.S. bases sections, running it as two short paragraphs. Both outlets quoted Larijani directly, citing state television Sedavesima as their source, and neither included reporter commentary or additional analysis; there is no contradiction between them, only differences in emphasis and story length.
