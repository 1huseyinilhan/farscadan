An explosion at the HABAS Steel mill in Aliağa, a district of Izmir, on Monday, November 9, has killed 17 people. According to IRNA's report citing Turkey's Health Minister Kemal Memişoğlu's statement, the death toll reached 17 in the latest update; many workers were also injured. The explosion occurred at midday at a facility in the Aliağa Heavy Industry Zone on the Aegean coast, triggering a large-scale rescue operation.

The event was first reported in the Persian press by Kayhan, drawing on sources from TRT, ISNA, and Xinhua. Kayhan's initial report said the explosion occurred when a ceiling crane broke and fell into the electric arc furnace during liquid steel-pouring operations; that account cited 6 deaths, 13 injured, and 10 people trapped under rubble. Images from the scene showed thick smoke rising from the facility, with fire brigades, medical personnel, and gendarme units dispatched to the area. According to Kayhan's account, Xinhua's initial assessment suggested the accident took place during liquid steel pouring. Hours later, IRNA reported, based on Turkish authorities, that the death toll had risen to 17; this update contained no explanation of the cause.

Turkish authorities' official statement differs: the explosion occurred when material fell during planned maintenance work and ruptured natural gas and oxygen lines. This account differs from the Persian sources' description of "a crane failure and liquid steel-pouring accident," so the exact cause remains **unconfirmed**. Turkey's Labor Minister Vedat Işıkhan stated that preliminary investigations supported this account. Justice Minister Akın Gürlek announced that six people had been detained and four prosecutors assigned to the investigation; the detainees' identities were not disclosed, and the presumption of innocence applies. President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan described the event as "a major workplace accident" and offered condolences.
