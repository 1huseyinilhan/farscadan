Suspicious unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) activity over RAF Lakenheath air base in Britain was confirmed Friday evening. According to Hamshahri, citing Sky News, a U.S. Air Force spokesperson announced that security personnel responded after observing unauthorized drone flights in the airspace above the base.

According to Hamshahri's report, this confirmation came after reports of alarms sounding at military bases in Britain; Sky News, citing a U.S. Air Force spokesperson, reported suspected UAV activity over Lakenheath. According to a separate IRNA report, a danger alarm also sounded Friday evening at RAF Feltwell, a Royal Air Force base in Britain. According to ISNA, the U.S. Air Force confirmed it had responded to suspected UAV activity over a military base in Britain. According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, officials asked citizens to immediately report any suspicious activity near the base to authorities.

According to Hamshahri, U.S. officials said the incident caused no damage to personnel, aircraft, or base infrastructure. According to Iran International, the U.S. acknowledged responding to suspected UAV activity over a British base but did not clarify which base or bases were affected or whether the source of the UAVs had been determined. Lakenheath, Mildenhall, and Feltwell bases are located near each other in the Suffolk-Norfolk region of eastern England and form part of the cluster of U.S. Air Force-operated bases; which of these bases or how many were affected by the same UAV wave was not fully consistent across sources, and this point remained unverified.
