The leaders of the United States, Russia, and China will hold a trilateral meeting Thursday in Shenzhen, China, as part of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit. According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, citing the New York Post, a White House official said the meeting would focus primarily on drafting an arms control agreement, with the war against Iran and the North Korea issue also potentially on the agenda.

According to IRNA, Donald Trump had previously mentioned the possibility of this three-way meeting among the world's three most nuclear-armed states. According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, Trump said in a statement Sunday: "I think one of the meetings with Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping is going to be very important," and added: "We're going to discuss a lot of things, including nuclear disarmament, but there are other various issues on the table as well. We're trying to end the war in Iran and we've done a good job on it." According to ISNA, citing U.S. media, Trump's previous in-person meeting with Putin was last August in Alaska, a gathering focused on efforts to end the Ukraine war; Trump also hosted Xi Jinping at the White House in September.

According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, China's Foreign Ministry stated that the 33rd APEC Leaders Summit would be held November 18-19, with the slogan "Building an Asia-Pacific Community for Shared Prosperity"; the APEC CEO Summit, bringing together business and government representatives, would occur November 17-18 preceding the leaders summit. APEC, comprising 21 Pacific economies, first held a leaders summit in 1989; China had previously hosted this summit twice—in Shanghai in 2001 and Beijing in 2014.

Whether the trilateral meeting would take place was not finalized when the report was published. According to Korea Times' November 7, 2026 report, China's Foreign Ministry confirmed that discussions with Washington and Moscow on a possible three-way leaders meeting were underway but noted that no consensus had yet been reached on the meeting's final format, composition of delegations, or agenda. If it occurs, this meeting would be the first trilateral summit where the three leaders meet together; the idea was first raised in September, when Kremlin spokesman Peskov disclosed that Putin and Trump discussed the possibility of holding such a meeting on the sidelines of the APEC summit.
