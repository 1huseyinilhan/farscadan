China's Foreign Ministry announced that U.S. President Donald Trump, Russian President Vladimir Putin, and Chinese President Xi Jinping will hold a three-way meeting on Thursday, Nov. 19, in Shenzhen, a city in southern China. According to ISNA's account, Beijing stated that this will be the first three-way summit among Chinese, U.S., and Russian leaders held in Shenzhen. According to IRNA's report, China's Foreign Ministry confirmed in a statement that Xi will hold a three-way meeting with his Russian and American counterparts.

The meeting coincides with sessions of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) leadership summit being held in Shenzhen. According to Iran International's report, China's Foreign Ministry spokesman stated that nuclear arms control would be among the important international topics on the meeting's agenda. The report emphasized that this will be the first three-way summit among Chinese, U.S., and Russian leaders held in Shenzhen.

According to IRNA's report, China's Foreign Ministry statement did not disclose the exact time of the meeting or a full agenda order; the spokesman only confirmed that the three leaders would meet and that the discussion would cover important international matters. In ISNA's report, it was noted that Trump will be meeting Xi face-to-face for the third time this year, Putin's participation was previously confirmed by Russia, and the U.S. formal confirmation of attendance was finalized with Beijing's announcement today.

## Context

**Background:**
- **Nov. 6, 2026:** Chinese Foreign Ministry spokesman Mao Ning confirmed for the first time publicly that discussions were underway among the three countries for such a meeting (Korea Times, Nov. 6, 2026).
- **Nov. 14, 2026:** Farsçadan reported in a previous story that a White House official said the summit would focus primarily on an arms control agreement.
- **Nov. 16, 2026 (today):** Beijing clarified the meeting's date (Nov. 19, Thursday) and location (Shenzhen) in an official statement.

**Who is who / concept:** *APEC* (Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation) is a regional forum of 21 Pacific-rim economies aimed at facilitating trade and investment; this year's leadership summit is being held in Shenzhen. Shenzhen, the host city, is located in southern China just north of Hong Kong and is known since the 1980s as China's first "special economic zone" for its rapid industrialization.

**By the numbers:**
- The APEC leadership summit is scheduled for Nov. 18–19, 2026, in Shenzhen (SCMP, 2026).
- China's Foreign Ministry first confirmed the three-way meeting publicly in a statement on Nov. 6, 2026; this was the first official confirmation the talks had received in public (Korea Times/AP, Nov. 6, 2026).

**What's next:** The meeting is scheduled for Thursday, Nov. 19. The exact agenda items have not yet been officially shared; according to SCMP's report, the parties are trying to establish a joint agenda on artificial intelligence and nuclear arms control before the summit. According to SCMP's reporting, Trump has stated publicly that he wants the meeting to address nuclear disarmament primarily, while Putin has prioritized bringing the Ukraine war to the agenda (SCMP, November 2026); this difference in priorities sustains uncertainty about whether the summit will produce concrete results.

**Fairness note:** Assessments of the summit's connection to the Iran-U.S. war were attributed directly to their source; no analysis or interpretation of intent was offered.

*Note: English-language reports from Nov. 6, 2026, indicated the summit had not yet been finalized, with Beijing's official voices taking a measured stance on tripartite disarmament talks, calling them not "fair, reasonable, or realistic" at this stage; today's Iranian press reports convey that Beijing is now confirming the meeting directly by providing a date and location.*
