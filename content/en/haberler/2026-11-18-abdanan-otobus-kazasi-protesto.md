According to Ettelaat, on November 14 (23rd of Aban) in Ilam Province, a bus carrying workers on the Dasht-i Abbas-Abdanan road overturned, resulting in the deaths of 13 engineers working on the Dehloran gas processing project. According to Kurdistan24's English report, some international news organizations reported the death toll as 12 with 16 injured; some of the wounded were reported in serious condition, so the death toll has changed over time. The exact death toll has **not been independently confirmed** across sources.

According to Ettelaat's report, the head of Ilam Road Police stated that three factors played a role in the crash: driver negligence, the rental company's violation of regulations in the rental agreement, and the road maintenance authority's failure to ensure safety on the route. In the same report, Ilam's chief prosecutor is stated to have filed charges against the company's general manager and the province's General Director of Road Maintenance and Land Transportation; it was noted that after the investigation is completed and responsibility is clarified, legal action will be pursued "without leniency." According to a separate Ettelaat report, with the presidency's special oversight, NGL Dehloran Petrochemical facility manager Yunus Karimi was removed from office.

According to Ettelaat's account, following the crash, a protest gathering began at Vali-e Asr Square in Abdanan; participant numbers initially ranged from 200–300 people but later grew to approximately 1,000, with protesters chanting "today is a day of mourning" and "resign, resign." As crowds moved from the square to other streets, some chants took on a political tone; it was reported that some individuals attempted to damage public property, but that the majority did not join in such actions; police were present at the scene to maintain order. According to Iran International's account, human rights organizations reported that at least 6 people were arrested in Abdanan following the protests; this figure was not confirmed by official authorities. Reza Pahlavi, the exiled former heir apparent, posted a video message saying he found the people of Abdanan "praiseworthy"; this is an assessment by an opposition political figure and has been separately attributed so as not to affect the report's neutrality.

## Context

**Background:**
- **November 14, 2026 (23rd of Aban):** A bus carrying Dehloran gas facility workers overturned on the Dasht-i Abbas-Abdanan road.
- **Same day:** President Masoud Pezeshkian instructed the Interior Minister to investigate the causes of the accident; mourning was declared in Ilam Province and in Abdanan (Kurdistan24).
- **November 17, 2026:** Ilam's chief prosecutor filed charges against the company general manager and the road authority general director; NGL Dehloran facility manager was removed from office (Ettelaat).
- **November 17, 2026 (today):** Protests gathered in Abdanan; human rights organizations reported arrests (Ettelaat, Iran International).

**Who is who / concepts:** *Dasht-i Abbas* (Dasht-e Abbas) is a region in Ilam Province subordinate to Abdanan County through which the crash route passed. *NGL* (Natural Gas Liquids) refers to a category of petrochemicals including propane, butane, and other liquid hydrocarbons separated from natural gas; the Dehloran facility processes these products.

**By the numbers:**
- According to Ettelaat, 13 people died in the crash; international reports cited by Kurdistan24 put the figure at 12 dead and 16 injured.
- According to Iran International, at least 6 people were arrested following the protests.
- According to Ettelaat's observation, the protest grew from an initial core of 200–300 people to approximately 1,000.

**What's next:** The investigation by Ilam's prosecutor and the outcome of the charges against company and road authority officials have not yet been announced; the **presumption of innocence applies** to those individuals. The protest continued the next day; Kayhan linked the demonstrations to "enemy" interference, while opposition media carried solidarity messages ([Farsçadan](../2026-11-19-abdanan-protesto-kayhan-dusman/)).

*Based on information available to this report, the removal from office and charges related to the crash are administrative and legal steps announced by official authorities; figures on protests and arrest numbers vary by source.*
