Concurrent court hearings took place Friday (November 20) in New York and London in the RAF Fairford air base attack plot case. According to Iran International, in Manhattan federal court, defendant M.B.S.A. repeatedly disrupted the hearing shouting in Arabic: "We are the army of Qasem Soleimani"; on the same day in London's Old Bailey, lawyers for the two defendants told the court they would likely not accept the charges.

According to the Telegraph report, M.B.S.A. appeared in prison clothing, stood up, shouted in Arabic, and repeatedly interrupted proceedings; he stated "our country is Iran and our great leader [Mujtaba] Khamenei and we are the army of Qasem Soleimani." Qasem Soleimani was the former commander of the Quds Force and was killed in a U.S. strike on Baghdad Airport on January 3, 2020 (December 13, 1398). Telegraph identified M.B.S.A. as one of Soleimani's supporters and wrote that photographs of the two together appear in FBI evidence. According to U.S. prosecutors, while detained, M.B.S.A. continued coordinating through coded language in phone calls with family members the attack plan on RAF Fairford scheduled for September 27 (Mehr 5).

Concurrently with M.B.S.A.'s New York hearing, the London phase of the Fairford case was also heard the same day at Old Bailey. According to Iran International, one defendant is reportedly Iranian and the other English, and both allegedly aided the IRGC's intelligence service; however, according to both the U.K. Crown Prosecution Service (CPS) statement and Al Jazeera's report, both defendants are British nationals — this detail about defendants' nationality **remains unconfirmed**. CPS states that defendants M.A. (24) and N.E. (25) attended a preliminary hearing via video link; M.A. is charged under Section 3(2) of the U.K. National Security Act with actions between August 31 and September 28 that could provide substantial assistance to a foreign intelligence service, while N.E. is charged under Section 4(1) for unlawfully entering a restricted area on August 22. According to the judge's ruling, the main trial will only begin a year later, on November 22, 2027. **The presumption of innocence applies.**

According to revised charges, the network consisting of M.B.S.A., M.M., and M.A.H., using the name "Harkat-i Ashab ul-Yamin al-Islamiya," claimed responsibility for 18 attacks across Europe in spring 2026; seven occurred in the UK and included arson, explosive devices, and knife attacks on two Jewish men in London. FBI Director Kash Patel characterized the charges against M.B.S.A. as evidence of "Iran's determination to conduct terror attacks in Europe and the U.S." SDNY Prosecutor Jay Clayton stated that the defendants "coordinated attacks targeting civilians in partnership with Iran-backed terrorist organizations." When initial suspects near Fairford were released in September after no explosives were found, Trump said he was "surprised" by the decision, while Secretary of State Rubio stated there was "no direct connection" between the suspect detention and the withdrawal of U.S. bomber aircraft from the base.

## Context

**Background:**
- **September 27, 2026:** Three suspicious vehicles near RAF Fairford prompted detentions; no explosives were found, initial suspects were released on bail ([29-country Fairford statement](../2026-10-13-fairford-29-ulke-bildirisi-ingiltere-yaptirim/)).
- **October 19, 2026:** Secretary of State Rubio said the U.S. supports the U.K.'s assessment holding Iran responsible for Fairford ([Rubio: Fairford, we agree with UK](../2026-10-19-fairford-iran-plan-rubio-ingiltere/)).
- **October 27, 2026:** CPS charged M.A. and N.E. under the National Security Act; the pair appeared at Westminster Magistrates' Court for the first time.
- **November 10, 2026:** The U.S. Department of Justice announced revised charges encompassing M.B.S.A.; it emerged that M.B.S.A. had been [arrested in Turkey earlier this year](../2026-11-11-abd-iran-teror-suclamasi-turkiye-tutuklama/).
- **November 20, 2026:** M.B.S.A.'s New York hearing and M.A./N.E.'s London preliminary hearing on the same day.

**Who and what:** *Old Bailey*, officially the Central Criminal Court, is London's principal criminal court. *SDNY* (Southern District of New York) is the U.S. federal prosecutor for New York's southern district, where M.B.S.A.'s trial is being heard.

**By the numbers:**
- The revised indictment against M.B.S.A. contains eight counts; some could result in life imprisonment (U.S. Department of Justice SDNY Prosecutor's Office, November 10, 2026 statement).
- CPS's October 27, 2026 statement indicates M.A. is charged with actions between August 31 and September 28, 2026, under Section 3(2); N.E. is charged with an August 22, 2026 action under Section 4(1).
- London trial date set for November 22, 2027 — more than a year after initial charges (CPS, October 27, 2026).

**Turkey dimension:** M.B.S.A. was arrested at a hotel in Istanbul earlier this year and brought to New York under FBI supervision; this is the only confirmed link of the investigation to Turkey. As reported in the [November 11, 2026 article](../2026-11-11-abd-iran-teror-suclamasi-turkiye-tutuklama/), few details about Turkish authorities' contribution to the arrest operation have been disclosed to the public; U.S. officials also have not fully explained the circumstances of the detention.

**What's next:** The main trial in London will begin November 22, 2027 (CPS). The next hearing date in the M.B.S.A., M.M., and M.A.H. case in New York has not yet been announced publicly.
