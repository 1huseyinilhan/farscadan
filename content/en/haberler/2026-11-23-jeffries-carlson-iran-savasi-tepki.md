House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries said Sunday evening on CBS News's "Face the Nation" that the U.S. must end the war against Iran, which he called "an expensive and arbitrary war we chose to wage." According to Ettelaat, Jeffries said the conflict has driven up prices for gasoline, food, and diesel.

According to Ettelaat, Jeffries listed lowering the cost of living and holding the administration accountable for corruption among the steps to be addressed in the new congressional session; he announced that Democrats plan to introduce a measure banning congressional stock trading. The same day, IRNA and ISNA reported that an unnamed Democratic senator called for Trump to be held accountable for "warmongering" against Iran and for cutting off war funding; IRNA and ISNA's pages were inaccessible to verify the report details, so the senator's identity and the full text of the demand remain unconfirmed.

Criticism also came from conservative quarters. According to Khabaronline, host Tucker Carlson and former national security official Joe Kent said on their program that the Iran war was a primary reason for Republicans' heavy midterm losses and the current economic crisis. Kent pointed out that diesel prices had risen to $6 per gallon and that Democrats had won majorities in both chambers of Congress. Carlson, naming Foreign Minister Rubio, CIA Director Ratcliffe, Defense Secretary Hegseth, and CENTCOM Commander Cooper as war supporters, called for their immediate replacement; he alleged that Mossad has fed unconfirmed intelligence directly to Trump and promised that if Trump followed Israel's lead and eliminated Iran's leader, the regime would collapse rapidly.

## Context

**Background:**
- **June 2025:** During a 12-day war, the U.S. conducted "Midnight Hammer" operations striking Iran's nuclear facilities.
- **November 20, 2026:** The U.S. Senate again rejected a resolution limiting Trump's war authority against Iran (see [U.S. Senate rejects war authority](../2026-11-20-abd-senato-iran-savas-yetkileri-reddi/)).
- **November 2026 midterms:** Democrats won majorities in both the House and Senate; according to Khabaronline, Carlson and Kent directly linked this outcome to the Iran war.

**Who and what:** Hakeem Jeffries is the Democratic Party leader in the U.S. House of Representatives. Tucker Carlson is a conservative commentator and host; Joe Kent is a former Special Forces officer and national security commentator who briefly served at the National Counterterrorism Center.

**By the numbers:**
- **$6:** The price per gallon of diesel that Kent cited as being reached in the U.S. (Khabaronline, November 23, 2026).
- **2 chambers:** The number of Congress chambers—House and Senate—that Kent said Democrats won in November midterms (Khabaronline, November 23, 2026).

**What's next:** Jeffries said steps to end the war would be addressed in the new congressional session and announced plans to introduce a measure banning congressional stock trading; no specific date was given.
