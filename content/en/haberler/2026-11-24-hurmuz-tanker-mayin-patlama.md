According to Hamshahri, Iran's Revolutionary Guards Navy announced that three tankers in Strait of Hormuz waters struck naval mines and caught fire severely. In a statement from the IRGC Navy, it said that in recent hours, three tankers traveling in "unauthorized routes" hit mines and burst into flames following the explosions.

The statement claimed that one tanker off the southern coast and two others in the eastern strait struck mines "while under intense U.S. military air support and escort." The IRGC Navy stated that U.S. air escort proved unable to prevent the events and announced that images of the explosion and fire would be released "in coming hours." The statement, addressed to ship owners, captains, and maritime companies, said: "The Strait of Hormuz is not a place where unauthorized routes are attempted or maritime warnings are ignored." It emphasized that U.S. military escort cannot replace compliance with the instructions of the IRGC Navy's traffic control center and maritime regulations. The statement concluded by warning that unauthorized transit and ignoring warnings would be counted as "violations" and "collusion with hostile forces," and would be met with "resolute response." The IRGC Navy did not specify who laid the mines; as of the time this report was prepared, no independent maritime monitoring organization or third party had confirmed the incident.
