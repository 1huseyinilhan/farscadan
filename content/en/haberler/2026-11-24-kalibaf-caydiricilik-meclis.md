According to IRNA, Parliament Speaker Mohammad Baqer Kalibaf said the way out of the "neither war nor peace" situation lies in achieving permanent deterrence, stating: "If we reach permanent deterrence, we will be strengthened; we must recognize and implement its components across every field—economics, politics, military, media, and social cohesion." Kalibaf made this statement on Monday, November 23, at the closing ceremony of the "Third Hope Media Event" at Parliament.

According to Donya-ye Eghtesad, Kalibaf said "the enemy wants to impose a grinding 'neither war, nor peace' situation on us" and added that "there are military ways to break the blockade, which we will advance in the near future." Kalibaf also stated that "when three great powers sit side by side, the enemy can no longer bargain over Iran." According to Khabaronline, Kalibaf said in the same speech that "today Iran is undoubtedly among the world's first four powers" and that "deterrence means Iran's influence reaches the top five powers' decisions even without sitting at their summit." Khabaronline reported Kalibaf saying that diplomacy and war are two tools that must be used together to reach deterrence capacity, and that "military struggle alone cannot lift the shadow of war, nor can diplomacy alone remove this threat."
