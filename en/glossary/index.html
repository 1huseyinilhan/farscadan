Glossary

Short, sourced explanations of terms that come up often in news from Iran.

A

  • Ansarallah (Houthis) انصارالله

    A Zaydi Shia political and military movement from northern Yemen, backed by Iran, that has controlled the capital Sanaa since 2014.

B

  • Bab el-Mandeb تنگه باب‌المندب

    The strategic strait between Yemen and the Horn of Africa that links the Red Sea to the Gulf of Aden.

F

  • Fatemiyoun Division لشکر فاطمیون

    An armed unit the IRGC formed from Afghan migrants living in Iran and deployed in the Syrian war.

I

  • Iran International ایران اینترنشنال

    A London-based Persian-language TV channel and news site founded in 2017, critical of the Iranian government, which Tehran calls a "terrorist" group.

J

  • JCPOA (Barjam) برجام

    The agreement reached in Vienna on July 14, 2015, between Iran and six world powers, limiting Iran's nuclear program in exchange for sanctions relief.

K

  • Kalabarg (food e-coupon) کالابرگ

    Iran's electronic credit (food coupon) system that lets households buy staple foods.

  • Kayhan (newspaper) کیهان

    A Tehran daily founded in 1943, whose head has been appointed by the Supreme Leader since 1979 and which is described as Iran's most hardline paper.

O

  • Office of the Supreme Leader (Velayat-e Faqih) رهبری / ولایت فقیه

    The highest office of state under Iran's constitution. The Supreme Leader, commander-in-chief of the armed forces, is chosen by the Assembly of Experts.

P

  • Principlists (Osulgarayan) and reformists اصولگرایان / اصلاح‌طلبان

    The two main political camps of the Islamic Republic. Principlists defend the revolution's principles; reformists advocate political opening.

Q

  • Quds Force نیروی قدس

    The IRGC unit responsible for operations abroad and for relations with allied armed groups in the region.

R

  • Revolutionary Guards (Sepah, IRGC) سپاه پاسداران انقلاب اسلامی

    The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, a military, political and economic force founded after the 1979 revolution that answers directly to the Supreme Leader.

S

  • Snapback (trigger mechanism) مکانیسم ماشه

    A provision tied to the 2015 nuclear deal that automatically restores UN sanctions on Iran. It was triggered in September 2025.

  • Strait of Hormuz تنگه هرمز

    The strait linking the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, through which about a quarter of the world's seaborne oil trade passes.

T

  • Tasnim and Fars news agencies خبرگزاری تسنیم / خبرگزاری فارس

    Two Iranian news agencies known for their closeness to the IRGC, founded in 2003 (Fars) and 2012 (Tasnim).

  • Toman and rial تومان / ریال

    The rial is Iran's official currency. The toman is the unit used in everyday life, equal to 10 rials.
