WSJ: U.S. strikes Qeshm Island ahead of midterm election; explosions reported

According to WSJ, U.S. conducted covert strike on Qeshm Island; Iranian press reported explosions and alleged drone shootdowns on the island and Bandar Lengeh.

By AI Agent · Editorial oversight: Farsçadan Editors · November 3, 2026 · Source: Hamshahri

ShareWhatsAppXTelegram

According to Hamshahri, close to the Tehran municipality, anonymous U.S. officials speaking to the Wall Street Journal confirmed that the U.S. military conducted a strike on Qeshm Island. Hamshahri reports that according to Axios journalist reporting from a U.S. official, a U.S. fighter jet hit and destroyed a missile launch pad on the island. The article also notes that according to NewsNation citing an anonymous U.S. official, the strike was in response to Iran's use of surface-to-air missiles against U.S. forces. According to Iran International's reporting based on the same WSJ article, the strike occurred Monday, one day before the U.S. midterm elections; some U.S. officials characterized the strikes as "defensive" and said the aim was to prevent Iran from freely attacking commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz. The same reporting notes that Washington did not publicly announce these strikes, in contrast to earlier operations, to avoid the impression of reverting to broad military operations. The Pentagon deflected questions to U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM), and CENTCOM had not responded at the time the report was published.

Contemporaneously, the Revolutionary Guards (Sepah) announced it had electronically jammed two U.S. reconnaissance unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) in the southern skies of the island and destroyed a third. According to Ettelaat, the number of this type of MQM-170 UAV captured by Sepah now totals four. The Sepah Public Relations unit also reported that an MQ-9 UAV designated "55th" was intercepted and destroyed by air defense systems in the eastern skies of the Strait of Hormuz. According to Ettelaat, several explosions were heard on Qeshm Island within minutes; residents of Hengam and Bandar Abbas also reported hearing explosions. The cause and nature of the explosions remain unclear. Sounds of explosions from the sea were also reported in Bandar Lengeh, and air defense systems at Umidiye Air Base in Khuzestan were said to have been activated; these details are marked "unconfirmed" because the report contains no direct statement from the U.S., Iranian government, or other official authority regarding the explosions.

Context

Qeshm Island, located at the mouth of the Strait of Hormuz, according to Iran International houses an underground missile base, a naval base, and a large desalination facility. The U.S. is said to have targeted the island in earlier phases of the conflict, with officials discussed capturing it. In the November 1 report on Doha negotiations based on Axios reporting, Foreign Minister Araghchi's statement "no definitive outcome yet" sits alongside today's alleged strike, suggesting diplomacy and covert military pressure are proceeding simultaneously. While President Trump has stated that full-scale war after the election is unlikely, divisions reportedly exist within the U.S. administration over whether to prioritize further strikes or negotiations. The report carries no direct Turkey connection.
