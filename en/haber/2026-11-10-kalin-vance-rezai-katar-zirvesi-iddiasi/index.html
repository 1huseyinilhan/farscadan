Turkish intelligence chief: Vance, Rezaei to meet in Qatar next week; Tehran denies

Turkey's intelligence chief says VP Vance and Iran's security council secretary will meet in Qatar; IRGC-affiliated agency denies the claim.

By AI Agent · Editorial oversight: Farsçadan Editors · November 10, 2026 · Source: Iran International

ShareWhatsAppXTelegram

İbrahim Kalın, head of Turkey's National Intelligence Organization (MIT), announced that U.S. Vice President JD Vance and Iran's Supreme National Security Council Secretary Mohsen Rezaei will meet next week in Qatar. According to Iran International, the IRGC-affiliated Tasnim news agency countered, citing a senior security official in Tehran, that no such meeting is planned.

At a conference in Ankara on Monday, Kalın stated that Qatar will host Vance and Rezaei, and that the meeting could advance negotiations between Washington and Tehran. According to the first Turkish newspaper to report it, Kalın also warned that if no agreement is reached, the U.S. could launch a new attack on Iran. The same day, Ettelaat newspaper published a headline: "Vance and Rezaei will meet next week in Qatar."

Shortly afterward, Tasnim, speaking through a senior security official in Tehran, denied that such a meeting is planned; the official stated that Iran's position had not changed and that no meeting was foreseen with Washington unless the U.S. practically fulfilled seven conditions. Ettelaat also reported this "rapid response" from the IRGC-linked news agency in a separate story. Qatar, meanwhile, is pursuing mediation on a framework intended to reduce the U.S. naval blockade and unfreeze Iranian funds in exchange for Tehran allowing nuclear inspections and reopening the Strait of Hormuz.

Context

Background: Qatar's mediation effort has been ongoing for months:

  • October–November 2026: Qatar's Prime Minister worked to broker a framework with the U.S. and Iran involving easing the naval blockade and unfreezing funds in exchange for nuclear inspection and reopening the strait.
  • November 9, 2026: Following Qatar's PM meetings with Trump and Vance in Washington, Trump said it was a "good conversation"; Rezaei the same day called the negotiation claims "fictional" (Rubio: Iran's demands unacceptable).
  • November 9, 2026 (Ankara): Kalın announced the Vance-Rezaei meeting would take place in Qatar.
  • November 9, 2026 (Tehran): Tasnim denied the claim.

Who and what: İbrahim Kalın is head of Turkey's National Intelligence Organization (MIT) and a former chief presidential advisor. Mohsen Rezaei is Iran's Supreme National Security Council Secretary and former IRGC commander; he leads the institution known in Persian as Şura-yı Ali-ye Amniyet-i Melli (Supreme National Security Council). In a separate statement the same day, Rezaei told the U.S. it was time to "surrender before seven conditions" and called the negotiation claims "fictional propaganda."

By the numbers: Kalın's mentioned meeting was scheduled for "next week"; the seven conditions Tehran has been publicly laying out for months form the core of its stated position. According to APA.az and MTV Lebanon, Tehran's denial came from a single unnamed "senior security official"; no official government spokesman issued a separate statement on the matter.

Turkey dimension: The claim originates directly from a Turkish state official, making it directly relevant to Turkey: Ankara is positioned as a channel for information between Washington and Tehran and as a mediator in negotiations. English-language sources such as APA.az and MTV Lebanon also reported the story based on Kalın's statement but included Tehran's denial.

What's next: Whether the Doha meeting Kalın mentioned will actually take place "next week" remains uncertain; Tehran's official position is that no meeting is planned unless the U.S. meets the seven conditions in practice.
