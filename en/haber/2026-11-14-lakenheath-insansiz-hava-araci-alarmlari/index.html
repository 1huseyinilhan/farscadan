U.S. bases in Britain on alert—suspicious drone activity confirmed over Lakenheath

Suspicious unmanned aerial vehicle activity confirmed over Lakenheath air base in Britain; U.S. Air Force security deployed.

By AI Agent · Editorial oversight: Farsçadan Editors · November 14, 2026 · Source: Hamshahri

ShareWhatsAppXTelegram

Suspicious unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) activity over RAF Lakenheath air base in Britain was confirmed Friday evening. According to Hamshahri, citing Sky News, a U.S. Air Force spokesperson announced that security personnel responded after observing unauthorized drone flights in the airspace above the base.

According to Hamshahri's report, this confirmation came after reports of alarms sounding at military bases in Britain; Sky News, citing a U.S. Air Force spokesperson, reported suspected UAV activity over Lakenheath. According to a separate IRNA report, a danger alarm also sounded Friday evening at RAF Feltwell, a Royal Air Force base in Britain. According to ISNA, the U.S. Air Force confirmed it had responded to suspected UAV activity over a military base in Britain. According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, officials asked citizens to immediately report any suspicious activity near the base to authorities.

According to Hamshahri, U.S. officials said the incident caused no damage to personnel, aircraft, or base infrastructure. According to Iran International, the U.S. acknowledged responding to suspected UAV activity over a British base but did not clarify which base or bases were affected or whether the source of the UAVs had been determined. Lakenheath, Mildenhall, and Feltwell bases are located near each other in the Suffolk-Norfolk region of eastern England and form part of the cluster of U.S. Air Force-operated bases; which of these bases or how many were affected by the same UAV wave was not fully consistent across sources, and this point remained unverified.

Context

Background:

  • September 27, 2026: A suspicious security incident occurred at RAF Fairford base, which Britain's Prime Minister characterized as "probably Iran-directed."
  • November 9, 2026: Enhanced security measures began at RAF Lakenheath and RAF Mildenhall without explanation; authorities did not clarify whether this was connected to the Fairford investigation (TWZ, November 9, 2026).
  • November 13, 2026: A danger alarm sounded at RAF Feltwell; the same evening, suspicious UAV activity over RAF Lakenheath was confirmed by the U.S. Air Force.

Key terms: RAF Lakenheath, a Royal Air Force base in Suffolk in eastern England that hosts U.S. Air Force F-35A fighter aircraft; it is part of the U.S. military presence in the region alongside RAF Mildenhall and RAF Feltwell.

By the numbers:

  • According to TWZ's November 9, 2026 report, enhanced security measures covered at least two bases (Lakenheath and Mildenhall).
  • G7 foreign ministers' joint statement issued November 13 referenced Britain's assessment holding Iran responsible for the September 27 Fairford incident (Donya-ye Eqtesad's November 14, 2026 report on the G7 statement).

What's next: U.S. and British authorities have not announced any determination about the source of the UAVs. Officials asked citizens to report any suspicious activity near the base; the outcome of the Fairford investigation and any connection to recent events has not been shared with the public.
