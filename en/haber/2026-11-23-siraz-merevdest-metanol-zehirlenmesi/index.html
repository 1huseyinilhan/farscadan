Methanol poisoning in Shiraz and Marvdasht: 30 hospitalized, 1 dead

In 24 hours, 30 people in Shiraz and Marvdasht were hospitalized with methanol poisoning, 1 died; death toll in Nishapur rose to 4.

By Farsçadan Editors

ShareWhatsAppXTelegram

In Shiraz, the capital of Fars Province, and in nearby Marvdasht, the number of people hospitalized with methanol poisoning symptoms in the past 24 hours reached 30, with one death. According to Hamshahri, the Assistant Deputy for Treatment at Shiraz University of Medical Sciences said the poisonings stemmed from homemade or illicit alcohol.

According to IRNA and ISNA reports with the same figures, poisoning cases arrived at Shiraz and Marvdasht hospitals within the past 24 hours. According to Hamshahri's report, the official noted that patients presenting with methanol (industrial alcohol) symptoms are being closely monitored. The same report recalled that homemade alcohol poisonings have been part of a series of incidents across the country: a similar wave occurred recently in Nishapur, where the number of poisoned reached 120 and deaths rose to 4; brain death was recorded in one patient, 11 patients were intubated, and 14 were in intensive care (see Alcohol poisoning in Nishapur). According to Donya-ye Eqtesad's report, authorities issued fresh warnings about methanol's fatal effects; whether the source of the Shiraz outbreak is a single batch of liquor or multiple distribution points has not been clarified in official statements—this point remains unconfirmed.

Context

Background:

  • Alcohol production, sale, and consumption are prohibited in Iran under the Islamic Penal Code; demand is met through unmonitored homemade or illicit production, creating risk of methanol contamination.
  • November 21, 2026: In Nishapur, 33 people were poisoned, 3 died (see Alcohol poisoning in Nishapur).
  • November 22, 2026 evening: The toll in Nishapur rose to 120 poisoned and 4 dead; brain death was reported in one patient (Hamshahri, November 23, 2026).
  • October 2024: According to AP, at least 26 people in Iran died from methanol-contaminated alcohol.

Who and what: Methanol is a type of toxic alcohol that can be used in bootleg production in place of or mixed with ethanol; even small doses can cause vision loss and death. Fars Province is in southern Iran; Shiraz is the provincial capital, and Marvdasht is a historic town near Shiraz.

By the numbers:

  • 30 people / 1 death: The 24-hour poisoning toll in Shiraz and Marvdasht (IRNA/ISNA/Hamshahri, November 23, 2026).
  • 120 people / 4 deaths: The updated toll in Nishapur for the same period (Hamshahri, November 23, 2026).

What's next: Authorities are investigating whether the poisoning source is a single batch or multiple distribution points; official findings have not yet been announced.
