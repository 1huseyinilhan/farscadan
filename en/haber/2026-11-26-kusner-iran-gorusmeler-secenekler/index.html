Kushner: We've met with Iranian representatives multiple times; all options are on Trump's table

Kushner said he has met multiple times with Iranian representatives; Trump is focused on Iran and all options remain under consideration.

By Farsçadan Editors

ShareWhatsAppXTelegram

According to Donya-ye Eqtesad's "site-khan" (press summary) section, citing Al Jazeera English, Jared Kushner, U.S. President Donald Trump's son-in-law and special envoy, stated in an interview on the "MD Meets" podcast with broadcaster Mathias Döpfner that he has met multiple times with Iranian representatives and that Trump is "highly focused" on the Iran issue.

According to the report, when asked whether Washington will take new military steps against Iran, Kushner replied, "The President always keeps all his options on the table"—implying that both diplomatic and military options remain under consideration. He also said Trump assembled a team to tackle the crisis but did not provide details on which names comprise this team. When asked about European allies' support for Iran policy, Kushner said, "Europeans want a lot from America," and indicated that Europe's response to the Iran issue does not influence his judgment. According to Donya-ye Eqtesad, Kushner also noted that under the supervision of Vice President JD Vance, he is working to help Trump achieve a resolution since the "aggressive war" against Iran began.

In another section of the interview, Kushner said Israel must move from a "perpetual war mentality" and focus on economic diplomacy; he cited Saudi Arabia and the UAE as examples, noting that when they visit Washington, they "come with 100 businesspeople and sign deals." He suggested that if the Gaza war ends peacefully, Israel and Egypt could cooperate on building data centers in the Sinai Peninsula. Kushner also addressed the Ukraine war: "We are living in an extremely dangerous period," he said, adding that while the war remains partly confined to Ukraine, it has effectively become a proxy war in which Europe finances Ukraine's defense against Russia, and a small miscalculation could vastly escalate the conflict. He claimed Russian leadership is "exercising restraint" and noted that the U.S. is trying to establish communication channels between Russia and Europe. Kushner also said the "misunderstanding" between NATO and Russia is not a new phenomenon and expressed hope that resolving the Ukraine crisis could eliminate some of these misunderstandings.

Donya-ye Eqtesad noted that the article itself is merely a summary of an interview carried by U.S. media and that the "site-khan" section reflects state media reporting—meaning the claims' accuracy derives directly not from Donya-ye Eqtesad but from Kushner's own words via Al Jazeera English. The interview provided no details about when or at what level Kushner met with Iranian officials. For this reason, the scope of the "multiple meetings" statement could not be independently verified at the time of this article's preparation and is noted as "unverified." Tehran has previously denied allegations of a direct "backchannel" with the Trump administration; whether today's interview will reignite this debate remains to be seen.

Context

Background:

  • February 2026: Kushner and special envoy Steve Witkoff met with Iranian officials at the residence of the Omani ambassador in Geneva.
  • June 2026: Trump sent Kushner and Witkoff to Doha to calm tensions in the Strait of Hormuz; during the same period, Witkoff was also tasked with U.S.-Russia-Ukraine talks (see Witkoff's trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi).
  • September 2026: A three-hour meeting between U.S. officials and a Tehran delegation was reported during UN General Assembly week in New York; Trump announced this as a sign of "a big decision" on Iran.
  • November 2026: Kushner's "MD Meets" interview coincided with Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan's statement that an Iran-U.S. agreement is more important than opening the Strait (related report).

Who and what: Jared Kushner is Trump's son-in-law and special envoy for Middle East affairs in his second term; he holds no formal cabinet position and derives his authority directly from Trump. Steve Witkoff serves as Trump's Middle East special envoy and works with Kushner on the Iran file; the two also served together in Russia-Ukraine negotiations.

By the numbers: According to Fox News, Kushner and Witkoff made contact with Iranian officials in at least three separate known meeting rounds in February (Geneva), and March and June (Doha) of 2026; this count includes only publicly disclosed meetings, and the exact number implied by Kushner's statement today of "multiple meetings" was not specified. According to Jewish Insider, Kushner said in March 2026, before the war, that Iran was "not serious" in negotiations. The September 2026 New York meeting was reported to have lasted three hours; it stands as the longest-duration publicly disclosed contact on record.

What's next: Kushner provided no timeline for when talks would continue; the interview only stated that contacts are ongoing and that Trump is keeping his options open. No official next meeting date has been announced by either side.
