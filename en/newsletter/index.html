Weekly Newsletter

The Farsçadan newsletter arrives every Sunday evening. It is free and, for now, written in Turkish.

What's inside?

  • The weekly analysis ("Haftanın Çerçevesi"): our original piece connecting the week's main threads in Iran's Persian-language press.
  • Key stories of the week: selected headlines with one-line summaries, each linking to the full story on our site.

Newsletter sign-up opens very soon.

After you enter your email, we send a confirmation email; your subscription starts when you click its link. Every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link. Details: Privacy.
